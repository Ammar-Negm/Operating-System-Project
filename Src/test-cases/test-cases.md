##### ***Scenario A — Basic Mixed Workload***

###### This scenario is used to evaluate the general performance of the scheduling algorithms under typical conditions.

##### ***Scenario B — Short-Job Heavy***

###### This scenario demonstrates why the Shortest Job First (SJF) algorithm performs efficiently when the workload mainly consists of short processes.

##### ***Scenario C — Fairness Case***

###### &#x20;This scenario is designed to show how the Round Robin (RR) algorithm distributes CPU time fairly among processes.

##### ***Scenario D — Long-Job Sensitivity***



###### One process has a very large burst time while the remaining processes are relatively short.

###### &#x20;This scenario highlights how different scheduling algorithms handle long processes compared to short ones.

##### ***Scenario E — Validation Case***



###### Invalid inputs are entered, such as a negative quantum value, negative arrival time, or a burst time equal to zero.

###### This scenario demonstrates that the system validates inputs correctly and displays appropriate error messages.
